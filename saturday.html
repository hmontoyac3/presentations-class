Three minutes, six parts

This is the architecture. You do not have to invent the shape as well.

  1. Hook — make us care. The first fifteen seconds, and the only part everyone hears
  2. Problem — show the current reality. Short, you told us yesterday
  3. Solution — what are you proposing? One thing
  4. Evidence — why might it work? A number, a comparable, a real case
  5. Contrast — before versus after. This is tip three, and it is where the pitch becomes a story
  6. Ask — what do you want from the room? Be specific, and say the number

Every pitch that fails today will fail in the same two places: it will spend ninety seconds on the problem, and it will end with "thank you" instead of an ask. A pitch that does not ask, does not get.

16.40 – 17.00 · what actually made people invest

Read your own reasons. Almost none of them are about the product.

"I understood who pays." "I remembered the example." "I believed her." Those are not judgements about a business. They are judgements about a presentation, and you made nine of them this afternoon without being taught how.

The five tips, this afternoon

You did not watch these work. You used them.

  1. Start with why they should care. Every pitch that opened with the person, not the product, got a vote
  2. One idea. The teams you remember are the teams you can describe in one sentence
  3. Contrast. Before and after. No number travelled alone this afternoon
  4. Structure before slides. You had no deck to hide behind, and it turned out you did not need one
  5. Delivery. The content froze at ten past one. Everything that improved after that was you

Five techniques is not what happened here. You persuaded a room of people who had no reason to say yes, and you did it in three minutes.
