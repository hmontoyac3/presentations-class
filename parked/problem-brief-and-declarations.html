13 minutes · four questions · one submission per group

Interrogate the problem until it has a name and a face.

  1. Who exactly has it? One person, with an age and a job. Not "companies"
  2. Who would pay to fix it? Often not the same person
  3. One real example, in thirty seconds
  4. Why now? What changed

Still no solution. If one comes to you, write it in the corner and turn it face down.
